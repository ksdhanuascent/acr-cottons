Meet Our Founder
The visionary behind ACR Prints is dedicated to redefining home comfort through artisanal excellence. Based in Erode, India, ACR Prints is a premium bedding brand focused on crafting elegant, soft bed spreads and pillow covers. Our collections are designed to bring a royal, peaceful feel to every home, blending traditional craftsmanship with modern luxury for a truly serene living experience.

From Thread to Treasure- crafting designs to make evolution in the the textile industry. 

Product Offerings: The catalog features high-end home textiles such as  jacquard pillows, velvet lumbar cushions, duvet covers, pillowcases, and bedspreads.
Subscriptions & Bundles: They offer curated cloth subscription boxes (like the Royal Neutral Bed Set Box and Bold & Vibrant Sheet Sets).

Localized Enterprise Identity: Operating out of Erode, Tamil Nadu (a prominent textile hub known as the "Textile Valley of South India"), the business naturally taps into local textile heritage and craftsmanship while maintaining an online storefront.

Boutique Hotel Aesthetic at Home: The core philosophy—"Textiles Woven for Everyday Luxury"—focuses on bringing the feel of a high-end boutique hotel into everyday residential spaces using rich colors, intricate patterns (like damask and jacquard), and soft fabrics.
